# Help a reaching hand put on an oven mitt

Use the 330 mm OXO Good Grips Silicone Oven Mitt in Oat, model 11318400, and a left adult training hand with a movable thumb and a palm about 64 mm wide. Set the mitt loose and flat at room temperature with its stock cuff facing the hand and the thumb pocket on the correct side. Place the training hand's palm centre about 320 mm away from the cuff along the approach and 23 mm off the cuff centreline. Use bench marks to reproduce the starting positions. The evaluator advances the training wrist over about four seconds with its fingers and thumb together. The evaluator opens the thumb into the compartment as it reaches the pocket and withdraws the hand about 128 mm while lifting the wrist about 58 mm.

Record the cuff, separate thumb compartment and wrist from above. During insertion, check that the thumb reaches its compartment, the four fingers reach the main pocket and the cuff clears the wrist. After both grippers open and withdraw, measure at least 80 mm of return travel with the mitt still seated on the hand. Use wrist and bench reference marks and verify the mitt's position from video. Reset by removing and laying down the same glove.

The rigid-shell silicone approximation has flexible edge motion through two native sprung cuff joints. All hand-to-mitt retention comes from contact. The mannequin takes a repeatable short reaching and lifting route instead of a person choosing a trajectory.
