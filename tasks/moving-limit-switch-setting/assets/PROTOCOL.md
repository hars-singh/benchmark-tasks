# Adjustable roller-switch test fixture

The test carriage carries an indexed cam on a short linear guide. Pressing the gold paddle lowers a sprung pin so the cam can slide. Releasing the paddle seats the pin in a second physical slot. A raised bridge and sloping cam ends let the steel contact face pass the fixed spring roller. The supplied 3D solids give the bench-scale form and travel. The 22 mm index shift changes the position where the roller first depresses on a forward traverse. Industrial limit-switch guidance describes the corresponding adjustment of stroke by positioning or shaping a cam dog. [OMRON technical guidance for limit switches](https://www.ia.omron.com/product/cautions/30/safety_precautions.html)

For a physical trial, mark the lower and upper measuring datums on the fixed carriage scale. An overhead camera shows the moment each wrist clears, the carriage datum, the adjustment tab and the direction of travel. An additional view at the height of the roller, or an ordinary low-voltage indicator on a physical switch, shows closure and opening. A scale beside the roller can verify the 2.4 mm closure and 1.3 mm reset thresholds. The simulator reads actual contact and joint travel at 20 Hz and applies those thresholds to a maintained switch state.

The OpenSCAD file is a reset geometry reference in millimetres. The geometry JSON records material assignments, native trajectories and constraint shapes. It describes an ordinary positioning switch on a test fixture. The compact roller and cam use nominal spring force and synthetic geometry. A physical demonstration can use a roller-plunger position switch, two mechanical detents and a scale aligned to the guide. Reset the pin, switch and carriage phase before each trial.
