# Reseat loose window-screen spline around one corner

Use a 226 by 254 mm aluminum vent-screen frame made from 5/16 by 3/4 inch rail. Fit standard Phifer fiberglass screen and continuous 0.140-inch Phifer vinyl spline. Seat the opposite two edges and the full adjoining rail beyond the corner. On the front run, seat the first 21 mm away from the corner. Leave the remainder and the full bend loose over the groove. Keep the mesh under the spline. Set the wooden Phifer roller beside the frame with its concave wheel toward the starting end.

Use a ruler, straightedge and close-up video to check every 20 mm and through the bend. The spline should sit no higher than 1 mm above the groove edge. After the roller moves away, check that the repaired run meets the seated adjoining rail and retains the fiberglass. Complete the check before trimming. Reset by lifting the same front run and bend, or fit fresh spline if it deforms.
