# Verify abrasive-belt tracking on a WEN 6502T

Mount the WEN 6502T on a stable bench using the normal bolt holes. Keep its fixed guards and work stop in their factory locations. Fit the manufacturer's 80-grit 4 by 36 inch abrasive belt in the correct direction, tension it, and lock the bed at horizontal. Set the belt approximately centered. Set the factory tracking knob slightly off its centered setting so a short observation shows 3.5 to 8 mm of drift inside the pulley width. Keep the keyed switch in its normal working condition.

Use the manufacturer's belt-tracking start-stop procedure. Observe the first short run from a fixed camera. Wait for the drums to stop before adjusting the external knob. Run again to check centering and stop it after the reading. Place a ruler beside the stationary guard to scale the video. Keep the ruler, hands and loose objects outside the abrasive path. Use the manufacturer instructions and clear the bench before powering the sander.

Record the belt edge relative to the center of the drive drum before and after adjustment. Record the factory switch at the end. The simulated model uses reduced drum velocity for visibility and derives the direction and amount of axial travel from the native tracking screw and the running condition. Use the stock machine at its normal speed in the physical trial.
