# Moving enclosure latch fixture

The fixture has a spring-supported lid and a steel eccentric cam. One hand presses the lid far enough for the second hand to turn the orange paddle past center. A closed stop holds the cam beyond center while its eccentric still bears down on the lid. The blue pad and the orange paddle are on opposite sides of the moving enclosure so the two arms can act concurrently. The mechanism has an 11.5 mm cam eccentricity, a 9 mm cam radius, a 55 mm handle offset and a 23-degree closed stop. The nominal effective lid spring stiffness is 1,150 N/m. Damping and travel limits are given with the geometry.

The OpenSCAD file shows the reset assembly in millimetres. The JSON gives all bodies, materials, actual spawn shapes, moving trajectory and fixture constraints. Build with the listed stock and a guided spring assembly. The commercial latch reference describes the over-center principle for enclosures. A side camera with a scale shows lid compression, latch angle and carriage travel after both hands move away.
