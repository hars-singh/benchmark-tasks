# Damping a lightweight load on a moving trolley

The 270 g suspended fixture represents a lightweight part carried through a station. The trolley moves along the gantry while the fixture swings about its native hinge. The arms must catch the guide tabs during active swing, settle the fixture toward vertical while moving with the trolley, and release the tabs. The remaining clear trolley travel distinguishes a stable release from a pose held in place by the grippers. Overhead handling equipment uses swing control to reduce load motion during trolley travel. [Konecranes sway-control example](https://investors.konecranes.com/press/konecranes-received-important-order-metal-company-boliden-1736697)

The JSON specifies the gantry, trolley, joint and colored guide tabs. The OpenSCAD dimensions are in millimetres. Reset the trolley at the center of the travel and use a removable 16-degree preset for the hinge. A side camera looking along the hinge axis and a printed vertical reference give the load angle. Use camera timestamps for the continuous under-grasp and hands-clear intervals. Put a ruler beside the guide to measure trolley displacement. The fixture remains connected at the hinge throughout.

The simulation models one plane of sway and a lightly damped rigid suspension. This bench test uses a low-mass fixture and two guide tabs. For an equivalent physical trial, use the drawn hinge and one-axis carriage with the specified mass and repeat the same starting angle and phase. Confirm the hands-off control still swings through the acquisition window before scoring robot damping.
