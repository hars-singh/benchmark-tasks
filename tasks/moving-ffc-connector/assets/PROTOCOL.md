# Guided ribbon connector assembly

The left handle drives the blue-green seater through a compliant clip. The right handle applies the fork to both latch tabs. The guided carriage continues to translate before and during assembly. The withdrawal instrument starts after both hands clear the handles and applies a calibrated pull to the actual clipped ribbon.

## Setup and reset

Use the geometry file for the port, contact follower, moving base, guided clip, latch, fork and seating yoke. Set the connector and clip on the carriage with the tip 0.8 mm upstream from the mouth, the latch fully open and both operators at their home positions. Start the 32 mm total native carriage stroke at phase zero.

## Recording

Record the seater stroke, latch stroke, carriage motion, arm withdrawal and retained ribbon insertion from the same trial. Use a graduated close view of the mouth and the two operator faces. For a physical equivalent use a separately calibrated withdrawal instrument limited to 0.65 N. Record its start after hand clearance and the insertion remaining through the proof hold. The simulator also checks actual opposing contact reactions at the clamp.
