# V60 water-handling trial

Fit a Hario V60 01 and its stock paper filter onto a small clear server. Rinse the paper and drain it before resetting the scale. Distribute 8.0 g of medium-ground coffee level in the filter. Mark the fixed dripper and kettle locations. Weigh the Hario V60 Drip Kettle AIR VKA-35-TB with 220 g of room-temperature water. Keep an overhead camera centred on the grounds and a view of the scale.

The first dose wets the centre and then the outer bed. Record the mass and an overhead photo, then time at least 24 seconds before the next pour. Measure the total dose from kettle mass lost and weigh the collected liquid after drainage. Photograph the final coffee bed and estimate the wet fraction inside the original outline using a transparent grid or pixel mask. Use a dry, preweighed bench mat under the station to quantify spills.

The simulator derives stream rate from kettle tilt and nozzle position, and computes wet coverage and drainage with a gravity stream and fixed retention model. The real measurement is a room-temperature practice pour, with the same dose, pause, coverage and catch criteria.
