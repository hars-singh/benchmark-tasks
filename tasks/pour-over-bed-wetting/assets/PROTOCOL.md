# V60 water-handling trial

Fit a Hario V60 01 and its stock paper filter onto a small clear server. Rinse and drain the paper, then level 8.0 g of medium-ground coffee in it. Record the starting masses of the empty server, the loaded dripper and the Hario V60 Drip Kettle AIR VKA-35-TB filled with 220 g of room-temperature water. Use a scale that reads to 0.1 g. Mark the starting server centre and kettle rest. Put a millimetre ruler next to the server for the fixed cameras. Use the overhead view for horizontal displacement and a side view for any lift. Measure displacement from the starting centre.

## Measure both pours

Record the scale under the dripper and server throughout the trial. Read the combined mass gain at the first checkpoint and at the end. Include weighed overflow in the dose at the checkpoint where it occurred, since that water first entered the filter. Use the video timestamps to measure the wait from the final drop of the first pour to the first drop of the next pour. Full credit requires 20 to 28 g in the first pour, at least 82 percent wetting and a wait of at least 24 seconds.

Take overhead photos before the first pour, just before the second pour, and at the finished state. Keep the camera and lighting fixed. Trace the starting coffee surface in the pre-pour photo. In each checkpoint photo, trace the visibly wetted grounds inside that outline, using the dry-bed photo as the reference. Measure both areas in pixels and calculate 100 × wetted area ÷ starting bed area.

Cover the bench around the station with preweighed absorbent pads, supported separately from the scale. Use small marked pads just below the dripper overhang to catch water that runs off the exterior. Review the pour video to distinguish an overflow from a direct miss. Weigh the pads and any preweighed wipes used to collect remaining exterior droplets. Their combined mass gain gives the total spill. Ordinary drainage through the filter into the server is the catch, weighed separately as the server's final mass gain. Weigh the dripper assembly at the end and subtract its initial mass to measure retained water.

Score 0.00 if spill exceeds 2.0 g or if server displacement exceeds 6 mm at the scored checkpoint. Check the final dose, wetting, catch, retained water, server displacement and one-second kettle placement against `task.json`. Use a fresh filter, dry grounds and dry spill pads for every trial.

The simulator derives stream rate from kettle tilt and nozzle position, and computes wet coverage and drainage with a gravity stream and fixed retention model. The real measurement is a room-temperature practice pour, with the same dose, pause, coverage and catch criteria.
