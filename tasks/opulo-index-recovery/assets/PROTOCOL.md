# Recover a missed index on an Opulo 12 mm feeder

Mount the Opulo LumenPnP v1.4 12 mm feeder on the stock loading station and begin in the normal tape-drive mode. Prepare the cover film using the stock peel gears and leave three consecutive loaded pockets exposed. Use unpowered MAX3443ECSA+T SOIC-8 parts in the original 12 mm embossed cut tape with 8 mm pocket pitch. These are the U21 DYNAMIXEL bus transceivers on the ROBOTIS OpenCR RevH controller used in TurtleBot3. Verify the feeder registration as part of the LumenPnP pick-position setup. Mark the first pocket on the side of the tape where the mark will remain outside the pick window. Leave the rest of the tape and the cover film prepared normally.

Use the stock pick reference and a fixed overhead close-up at 20 pixels/mm or more, calibrated with a small millimetre ruler in the tape plane. From the centered first position, tap forward twice before the trial. Each tap advances the carrier 2 mm. Record the actual reset offset. The robot recovers the first package using reverse indexing, then advances the second and third in order. Verify each stopped center independently from video and check that the packages never leave their pockets.
