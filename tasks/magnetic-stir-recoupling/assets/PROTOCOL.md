# Recover a decoupled magnetic stir bar

Use an IKA big squid white, part 0003672000, with the heating function absent. Fill one 150 mL DURAN SUPER DUTY 60 mm beaker with 115 mL of water. Add a small amount of blue food dye and use a 25 by 6 mm PTFE-coated stir bar. Set the motor to 200 rpm. Verify a stable reference with the beaker centered on the 160 mm plate. At reset, set the beaker 44 mm to the side with its base flat and confirm from overhead video that the offset bar has lost stable coupling.

The robot slides the vessel to the centre and releases it. Count three consecutive rotations in the target speed band for half credit. For full credit the glass stays centered within 7 mm and the bar stays within 10 mm of the plate centre at 170–230 rpm for ten continuous seconds with both hands clear. Use a scale in the overhead video and mark one end of the bar if needed to count signed turns. Score zero for spilling, lifting or removing the bar, inserting a gripper, or tilting the glass beyond 12 degrees.

The simulator models a separate driven motor rotor and a free stir bar. Native force and torque approximate magnetic attraction and phase coupling. Liquid drag is nominal and the water surface is visual. The preferred physical test checks spills directly.
