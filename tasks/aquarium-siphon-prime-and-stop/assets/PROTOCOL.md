# Prime and stop an Aqueon aquarium siphon

Use an Aqueon Mini 5-inch self-priming siphon kit 100106226, the kit hose and its bucket clip. Put washed inert gravel in a small test aquarium with water and no animals. Fill the aquarium to 123 mm above the work surface, including the 7 mm tank bottom. Start with the rigid intake upright on a bare patch of floor. The bucket fits the standard clip and has a 160 mm inside diameter. Its marks indicate 0.35 L and 0.45 L measured with a jug.

Put the intake at x=368 mm, y=148 mm in bench coordinates. The tank centre is x=390 mm, y=172 mm. The bucket centre is x=416 mm, y=-215 mm. Hold the hose end over the bucket, 43 mm above the bench. With the hose secured, the operator pumps the immersed intake up and down using the manufacturer’s self-priming procedure. Move the intake to a place just above the gravel and use the bucket line as the cue to lift the mouth above the aquarium waterline.

At the end, use the bucket marks to grade the volume. Use a side recording to check that the intake is at least 10 mm above the surface and flow has ceased for a full second. Count the same set of gravel pieces before and after. The simulator applies nominal head losses and uses the measured tube and gravel poses. The model assumes the supplied hose clip remains secure.
