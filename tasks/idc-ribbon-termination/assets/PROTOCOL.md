# Terminate the 20-way IDC end of a PreciseFlex pendant harness

The Brooks PreciseFlex G6000 remote front-panel port uses a 20-way IDC end on the manual-control pendant adapter. The manual names AMP 746285-4. Use TE Connectivity 1658621-4, the current replacement with the same 20-position, two-row interface, and 20-conductor 3M 3365/20 ribbon cable. The bench test evaluates the IDC end against a passive straight-through test adapter. Both ends stay disconnected from the controller and pendant throughout the trial. [Brooks manual, pp. 18 and 64](https://device.report/m/803a2fef01b86598ca02f7cf4e561b1563cb96c71d9431e12e63d487d6c30a44.pdf#page=71) · [TE connector and drawing](https://www.te.com/en/product-1658621-4.html)

Use the PanaVise 502, 506 IDC kit, 510 female socket block and 570 adjustable stop. Mount the press so the socket at x=463 mm and y=47 mm can be reached by both arms. Align the flat ram adapter with the socket. Set the depth stop against a correctly terminated reference of the same socket. Fit the open socket mating face down in the stock groove. Leave the cut ribbon end outside the slot, the far end in the passive test adapter and pin one toward positive y. Orient the red stripe to pin one during the trial. Replace the socket and cut ribbon sample each trial.

Photograph the registered ribbon from along the cable path. Award 0.50 for correct registration and a fully inserted end on an undamaged sample before complete closure. To award 1.00, also verify that the cap remains within 0.45 mm of the reference shut height and square within two degrees for one second. At that point, raise the platen and verify each pin end to end and check adjacent isolation with a cable tester or multimeter and passive 2 × 10 breakout headers. An open, crossed or shorted circuit on a completed termination scores 0.00. Incorrect registration or a damaged connector also scores 0.00.

The recorded simulation uses the same press with a 3M 89120-0101 two-row IDC socket and the same 1.27 mm ribbon pitch. The native cap, platen and lever measure the closing operation, and the articulated ribbon approximates flex under handling. Electrical measurements apply to the physical sample after closure.
