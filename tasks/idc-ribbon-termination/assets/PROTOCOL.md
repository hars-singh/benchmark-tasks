# Square and press a twenty-way IDC ribbon connection

Use a PanaVise 502 with its 506 IDC kit, 510 female socket block and 570 Posi-Stop. The press, kit and block list for about US$290 and the stop for about US$9. Mount the bench press so the socket is at x=463 mm and y=47 mm and the handle is accessible to the right arm. Keep the stock flat ram adapter square to the socket. Keep the mating face downward in the 6.35 mm stock groove and leave the cap above it. Use a preassembled 89120-0101 socket and matching twenty-conductor 3365 cable at 1.27 mm pitch. With a reference socket already terminated to the 10.7 mm nominal assembled height, bring the press down until the ram adapter touches the top of its cap and set the depth stop. Remove the reference and fit the open socket. Orient the red-striped edge toward pin one. Leave the cut end outside the socket and the far end connected to the switched-off tester.

Award half credit when the cable is oriented and fully through the slot, square to the contact strip. Use a close photo looking along the cable path. Award full credit at the set shut height if the cover stays even for one second and the registered ribbon stays in place. After the physical trial, raise the platen and test all twenty circuits and adjacent isolation. A reversed ribbon, damage, open circuit, or short makes the physical trial zero.

The simulation uses the physical handle, platen and cap position. The cap resistance and the two articulated cable sections represent the insertion load and cable flex. The simulation evaluates the twenty wire positions and mechanical coverage. The electrical cut-through and latch retention are tested on the real sample.
