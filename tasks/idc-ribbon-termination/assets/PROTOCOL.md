# Terminate the 20-way IDC end of a PreciseFlex pendant harness

The Brooks PreciseFlex G6000 remote front-panel port uses a 20-way IDC end on the manual-control pendant adapter. The manual names AMP 746285-4. Use TE Connectivity 1658621-4 with 20-conductor 3M 3365/20 ribbon. The ribbon uses 28 AWG stranded conductors at 1.27 mm pitch and a red stripe at pin one. The bench test evaluates the IDC end against a passive straight-through test adapter. Both ends stay disconnected from the controller and pendant throughout the trial. [Brooks manual, pp. 18 and 64](https://device.report/m/803a2fef01b86598ca02f7cf4e561b1563cb96c71d9431e12e63d487d6c30a44.pdf#page=71) · [TE connector and drawing](https://www.te.com/en/product-1658621-4.html) · [3M ribbon drawing](https://multimedia.3m.com/mws/media/22052O/3m-round-conductor-flat-cable-050in-3365-series-ts0080.pdf)

Bolt the TE 91085-2 manual arbor frame to the bench with the 768338-1 base and the 768341-1 receptacle tooling. Fit the RCPT upper tool to the ram and the lower insert to the base. Follow the manufacturer's ram-height adjustment, then close a reference socket, confirm its electrical connections and record its closed height. Set the handle for right-arm access with the socket at x=463 mm and y=47 mm. Seat the open socket mating face down in the lower insert with pin one toward positive y. Leave the square-cut ribbon end outside the open slot and the other end in the passive test adapter. Orient the red stripe to pin one during the trial. Replace the socket and cut ribbon sample each trial. [TE receptacle tooling instructions, 408-9831](https://www.te.com/commerce/DocumentDelivery/DDEController?Action=srchrtrv&DocFormat=pdf&DocLang=English&DocNm=408-9831&DocType=Specification+Or+Standard&PartCntxt=1658621-4) · [TE arbor frame instructions, 408-7777](https://www.ic-components.pl/files/70/91085-2.pdf)

Photograph the registered ribbon from along the cable path. Award 0.50 for correct registration with the cut end fully through the open slot on an undamaged sample. To award 1.00, also verify that the cap remains within 0.45 mm of the reference shut height and square within two degrees for one second. After complete closure, raise the platen and use a cable tester or multimeter with passive 2 × 10 breakout headers to check each pin end to end and check adjacent isolation. An open, crossed or shorted circuit on a completed termination scores 0.00. Incorrect registration, a detached cap, or a damaged connector or ribbon also scores 0.00.

The recorded simulation uses a PanaVise 502 lever press with a 3M 89120-0101 two-row IDC socket and the same 1.27 mm ribbon pitch. The native cap, platen and lever measure the closing operation, and the articulated ribbon approximates flex under handling. Electrical measurements apply to the physical sample after closure.
