# Seat a removable bridge and roll a loaded cart across it

Build both fixed decks and the plate from the geometry. Seat the plate so that it bears on both lips. Record the front and rear axles during the crossing, then verify the load and plate stayed in place. The cart is moved by the robot and carries a loose payload.
