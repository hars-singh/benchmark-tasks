# Eject a sheet remnant from an Epson LX-350

Prepare an Epson LX-350 using the manufacturer’s clearing-paper-jams procedure. The power is off, the cover is open, and the loose continuous supply is already separated from the retained remnant. Keep 29 mm of the 174 × 78 mm folded remnant below the platen. Provide a shallow 224 × 141 mm tray for the removed sheet. The test starts after the printer has been stopped and opened.

Use the original manual feed knob in the prescribed direction. Place a scale or two marks beside the paper path to measure advance before a gripper reaches the remnant. Once the paper has risen 23 mm, the robot may lift it free and lower it into the tray. The cover counts as closed within three degrees of the case’s normal seated position. Review the last continuous second with the sheet resting in the tray.

After the trial, verify normal feed using a clean sheet and the manual’s power-on procedure. Keep the knob operation restricted to the powered-off jam clearing phase. The native scene uses the physical platen, a spring-supported opposing roller and a free sheet with a rigid approximation of the folded thickness. No printed output or motor power is simulated.
