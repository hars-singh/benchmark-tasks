# MX-28AR horn installation check

Mount a ROBOTIS DYNAMIXEL MX-28AR securely with its output shaft vertical. Set its controller to the joint's neutral position, disable the drive and keep the stock washer in place. Put its HN07-N101 horn on a small parts mat where both factory witness marks can be seen. Keep the linkage off during the check.

Compare the witness marks with overhead video when the horn is seated and both hands are withdrawn. Measure the unloaded seated depth and rim position before the check. For a physical trial, an observer fits the original centre screw to the manufacturer specification after scoring the robot's seating and before enabling the output. Check both 25-degree positions and the return to centre at low speed with no attached load. Photograph the marks at each stationary point and check the rim and height again at neutral. The simulator uses direct spline contact and performs this movement on the clock.
