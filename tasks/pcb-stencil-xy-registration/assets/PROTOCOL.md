# Register a stencil to TurtleBot3's OpenCR RevH controller

Use a bare ROBOTIS OpenCR 1.0 RevH board fabricated from the manufacturer's OpenCR-Hardware release. Its full FR-4 outline is 105 mm by 75 mm, with 1.6 mm thickness for this setup. The RS-485 DYNAMIXEL interface uses a MAX3443ECSA+ at bottom-side position U21. For this selective printing stage, use the eight U21 lands. The supplied aperture Gerber traces the published mask and copper positions and defines eight 1.70 mm by 0.40 mm stencil windows. The eight copper lands measure approximately 2.10 mm by 0.60 mm with 1.27 mm lead pitch. Put the supplied artwork on 0.15 mm stainless foil in a 338 by 345 mm aluminium frame.

Clamp the board bottom-side up with the long axis parallel to X. In the bottom-up stencil view, the Gerber lower-left origin is the lower-left of the board outline. The included positions table gives the manufacturer coordinate and the reflected bottom-up coordinate. Center the outline at bench x=443 mm and y=−18 mm. Use an SMTmax YSJ-250 printer with the normal X and Y controls and board stops. Start with the board at −0.95 mm X and +0.70 mm Y from nominal. The foil starts closed and approximately 1.7 mm above the copper plane for dry inspection.

For physical scoring, mount a Plugable USB2-MICRO-250X on a rigid vertical stand over U21. Capture 1600×1200 stills at a fixed setting that resolves at least 40 pixels/mm. Use a two-axis microscope graticule with a certified 1 mm spacing across the same field at each of the two heights. Keep the camera mount fixed. Use a single aligned thru-reference on the fixed plate to tie the two in-plane coordinate systems together and correct the scale difference between the pad and foil planes. Check the transformed reference on both axes to within 0.03 mm before the run. Under this calibration the original 0.38 mm threshold spans at least 15 pixels. The 0.60 mm OpenCR lands use a tighter 0.15 mm registration tolerance, which spans at least six pixels.

Capture the bottom-side lands during the open inspection and the windows after reclosing, without moving the graticule reference or the board supports. Use ImageJ or equivalent to draw each land and opening center in calibrated millimetres and measure the largest center-to-center error. Take a matching closed calibration image at reset. Check opening with a side-view angle card. After closure confirm one second at the required alignment with both grippers clear of the rail.

The digital scene uses true U21 land positions matched between the copper and solder-mask artwork. It includes the complete bare-PCB outline and the larger exposed copper locations from the same bottom mask. This is a dry staging operation immediately before the assembly pass that fits the robot's bus transceiver.
