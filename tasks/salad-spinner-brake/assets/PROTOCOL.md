# Pump and brake an OXO salad spinner

Use the OXO Good Grips 32480 white salad spinner, with its clear 6.22-quart bowl, 4.95-quart basket, stock pump and built-in brake. Set it on a horizontal hard counter with its non-slip ring seated. Tear or cut 88 g of dry romaine into eight pieces. Mist the pieces with 23 g of water by mass and weigh them in the basket. Drain or wipe standing water from the outer bowl before closing the lid. Begin with all parts seated, pump unlocked and basket still.

Pump the knob and then use the brake while the basket is still turning. Record the visible basket from the side. Score the collected water by removing the stopped basket and weighing the outer bowl against its dry tare using a scale with 1 g resolution. Check the greens are retained in the basket. Record whether the brake arrested observable motion and whether the spinner stayed still for one second with the hands clear. Reset with dry bowl and basket and a fresh measured load.

The simulator uses native pump and brake travel and a rotating basket. A rate model driven by measured basket speed represents centrifuged water, and the visible water level follows the integrated collected mass. The rigid lettuce envelopes and reduced basket speed are approximations.
