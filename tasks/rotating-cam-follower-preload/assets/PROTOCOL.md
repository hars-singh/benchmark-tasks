# Set the preload on a rolling cam follower

Use the dimensioned stand and eccentric to reproduce the bench test. Rotate the cam at low speed and confirm the follower has clearance at part of the unloaded cycle. Operate the green adjuster, withdraw both hands and observe the follower over the full circumference.
