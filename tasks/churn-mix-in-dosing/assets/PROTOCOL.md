# Add chocolate chips late in the churn

Use the Cuisinart ICE-21P1 with its original 1.5-quart freezer bowl, paddle and locked clear lid. Prepare a thick vanilla base using the owner's instructions and begin the trial at the point for adding mix-ins, about five minutes before the planned stop. Use eighteen ordinary chocolate chips about 10 mm across. Put them in a plain quarter-cup measure on a small rubber rest and record the number and starting mass. Put a small reference mark on the outer edge of the freezer bowl where it can be seen through the lid.

Use side video to verify clearance between the measure and the lid and an overhead view to follow the chips. Check the countertop and measure for chips afterward, and count the number of uninterrupted bowl revolutions once the measure is withdrawn. At the end of the brief task, score the four quadrants from the overhead view while the bowl continues turning. Follow the maker's instructions for finishing the remainder of the recipe afterward.

The simulation moves individual chip bodies across a thick surface with native rotation and paddle contacts. Use a late-stage base in the physical trial so newly added chips stay visible for the short grading interval.
